# Name and logo

The code is open under the AGPL, but the name **Slopocalypse**, its logo and its store pages belong to the maintainer, Allan Kimmer Jensen. The code licence does not give you the right to use them.

You may:

- Fork the code, change it and release your fork under the AGPL, **with a different name and logo**.
- Say your fork is "based on Slopocalypse", or that it is compatible with it.
- Use the name to talk about the game: videos, streams, reviews, articles, mods, fan art.
- Run your own Slopocalypse servers with the unmodified game.

You may not:

- Publish the game or a fork under the name Slopocalypse, or a confusingly similar name, on Steam, itch.io, console stores or any other store or download site.
- Present a fork, a server or a product as official, or as made or endorsed by the maintainer.

When in doubt, open an issue and ask.
